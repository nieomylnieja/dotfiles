# Source

This skill adapts the
[STE writing kit](https://github.com/woosal1337/blog/tree/main/videos/ep01-the-cure-for-ai-slop)
from Ege Çelebi's
[episode about STE and AI writing](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uJblcC4lKYw).
The bundled linter is
[MIT-licensed upstream code](https://github.com/woosal1337/blog/blob/main/videos/ep01-the-cure-for-ai-slop/ste-lint.py)
with Nix adjustments.
